# Gene sets
- `ae_input_genes_3000.txt` — the 3,000 genes selected on the TCGA training partition (one-way ANOVA across the four immune–host subgroups, GEP genes excluded) and used as autoencoder input.
- `gep_genes.txt` — T-cell-inflamed GEP genes (Ayers 2017); see note inside on the 16 genes effectively scored.
- `marker_sets_fig3_supp6.csv` — five marker sets used in Figure 3 and Supplementary Figure 6 (31 genes, LDHA in two sets).
- `signatures_fig4c.csv` — ten signatures of the Figure 4C heatmap.
- `h.all.v2023.2.Hs.symbols.gmt` — MSigDB Hallmark collection (Liberzon et al. 2015), redistributed under CC BY 4.0 from https://www.gsea-msigdb.org/.
