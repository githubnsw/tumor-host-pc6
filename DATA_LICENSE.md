# Data license

The derived data in `data/`, `model/` and `results/` (per-patient body-composition measurements, GEP scores, autoencoder
latent features and PC scores, subgroup labels, gene lists, trained model parameters and result tables) are released under
the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Third-party data: TCGA and CPTAC-3 expression and clinical data are from the NCI Genomic Data Commons and are subject to
the NIH Genomic Data Sharing policy (open-access tier); CT images are from The Cancer Imaging Archive (TCIA) under the
collection-specific licenses; the MSigDB Hallmark gene sets are CC BY 4.0 (Broad Institute / UC San Diego).
The expression matrices in `data/expression/` are processed open-access GDC data (TCGA PanCanAtlas RSEM, CPTAC-3 STAR
TPM) restricted to the patients and genes analysed, provided for reproducibility.
